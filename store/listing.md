**App name (30):** Kenkosa Health
**Short description (80):** Track food, water, sleep, fasting, medication and mood in one app.
**Full description:**
Kenkosa Health brings the health habits you usually juggle across several apps into one place.

- Food diary: log meals, scan barcodes with your camera or search foods by name
- Water: one-tap logging that works offline
- Steps: automatic counting with your phone's step sensor
- Fasting: record fasts with a progress ring and history
- Sleep: log sleep and see a weekly chart against your target
- Medication: schedule medicines and mark doses as taken
- Mood and wellness: quick mood logging and guided meditation videos
- Goals and statistics: set goals and see your week
- Optional fingerprint/face lock to protect your data
- Available in English, Afrikaans and isiXhosa

Kenkosa Health is a wellness tracker and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
